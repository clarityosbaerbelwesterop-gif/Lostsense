# LOSTSENSE — Act VII Second Silence Production Plan

Act VII returns the campaign to changed surface geography and follows the canonical chain 80060–80064.

A Mercy Measured in Names is implemented as an explicit, persistent, mutually exclusive civilian-archive decision rather than an automatic quest tick. The choice has a portable versioned codec, deterministic restore validation, and is cross-validated against campaign progress so a save cannot claim the quest is complete without a choice.

The rewritten Gallows Library (20012) leads to Canon Sael Rhyne (30047). His source-level phases are Censor, Partial Silence and Private Archive. Suppression is represented by visible local seal geometry; the implementation does not model an opaque total control lock.

Sael's defeat advances 80064; the partial Second Silence remains a story consequence rather than being incorrectly prevented by victory.

This source pass does not claim UE 5.8 UHT/UBT/PIE, animation, VFX, audio, navigation, packaged-build or Pixel Streaming runtime validation.
