## 2. Major characters

### 3001 — Mara Venn

- **Silhouette:** short bellsmith coat, asymmetric shoulder harness, tuning hammer and folding survey staff.
- **Role:** central ally; first person to believe the Returned is physically real even when Bellgrave records disagree.
- **Motivation:** keep communities functional without lying about the deep crisis.
- **Arc:** technician → field partner → architect of distributed anchors in the finale.
- **Secret:** her family helped erase Odran from Bellwarden history after the Ninth Descent.
- **Gameplay function:** upgrades stabilization hubs, exposes event/persistence concepts through diegetic anchors, later supports boss-arena preparation.
- **Fate:** survives all canonical branches unless killed by a future explicit choice; becomes a principal endgame coordinator.

### 3002 — Kael Rook

- **Silhouette:** Charter marshal coat over practical plate; hooked survey axe; stamped brass face guard raised outside combat.
- **Role:** recurring rival and mine authority.
- **Motivation:** keep extraction running because half the March depends on Charter supply chains.
- **Arc:** antagonistic regulator → reluctant expedition partner → potential reformer or hardline corporate governor.
- **Secret:** signed rerouting orders for the Ninth Descent without knowing Oren's true destination.
- **Gameplay:** Charter contracts, mine access, pressure-tech rewards.
- **Fate:** choice-dependent political survival, not arbitrary betrayal.

### 3003 — Prelate Ilyr Vael

- **Silhouette:** tall white-ash vestments reinforced like field armor; mirrored face plate cracked vertically.
- **Role:** principal human antagonist through Act VIII.
- **Motivation:** end mass suffering by reducing contradictory memory load on the world.
- **Arc:** distant reformer → frighteningly effective stabilizer → author of the partial Second Silence → tragic late-game boss.
- **Secret:** Aster has been feeding him apparently objective catastrophe projections that omit alternatives.
- **Gameplay:** Synod enemy evolution, purification mechanics, late boss weapon line.
- **Fate:** defeated in Act VIII; exact death/survival presentation may branch but cannot remove Aster reveal.

### 3004 — Archivist Oren Sile

- **Silhouette:** narrow archive coat with layered paper-metal lamellae; lens array over one eye; never carries a heroic weapon.
- **Role:** mentor and misleading source of early historical truth.
- **Motivation:** prove a living unresolved witness can sever Aster before the Loom restarts.
- **Arc:** trusted scholar → increasingly evasive → exposed architect of Ninth Descent → possible atonement.
- **Secret:** selected the player and expedition as an experiment.
- **Gameplay:** lore decoding, Namarith interfaces, optional research upgrades.
- **Fate:** survives long enough to face consequences; no cheap off-screen absolution.

### 3005 — Tamsin Coil

- **Silhouette:** stacked tool belts, ceramic eye loupe, braided copper/witness-gold wire around one arm.
- **Role:** recurring crafter/merchant.
- **Motivation:** make dangerous old materials usable without letting factions monopolize them.
- **Arc:** local scavenger → mobile master craftsperson → founder of postgame salvage network.
- **Secret:** sells selected information to all factions to prevent any one from controlling supply.
- **Gameplay:** equipment crafting, sockets, boss-weapon conversion.
- **Fate:** persistent service character; can lose vendors/stock through regional outcomes but not silently vanish.

### 3006 — Ivo Salk

- **Silhouette:** former foreman with damaged lift-brace exoskeleton and a miner's lamp mounted at chest height.
- **Role:** mine guide and witness to the Ninth Descent launch.
- **Motivation:** atone for obeying reroute orders.
- **Arc:** fearful drunk → reliable deep guide → chooses to descend again voluntarily.
- **Secret:** heard the Returned's voice coming from below two days before the expedition entered.
- **Gameplay:** mine routes, lifts, environmental tutorials.
- **Fate:** may retire after Royal Deepworks or continue into Namarith support depending on side content.

### 3007 — Regent Solenne Mave

- **Silhouette:** black ceremonial half-plate with intentionally blank crest; crown chain worn at waist rather than head.
- **Role:** Crownless Court leader.
- **Motivation:** restore institutions people can trust even when historical legitimacy is unverifiable.
- **Arc:** defensive ruler → coalition builder or authoritarian restorer based on player evidence choices.
- **Secret:** has three mutually contradictory genealogies and knows none can be proven original.
- **Gameplay:** civic quests, heavy gear, political world-state changes.
- **Fate:** branch-dependent ruler, but Crownless Reach remains functional in all endings.

### 3008 — Serit No-Echo

- **Silhouette:** matte pale mantle with tactile knot-script; carries paired anchor spikes rather than conventional staff.
- **Role:** Quiet Assembly diplomat and Abyss guide.
- **Motivation:** prevent surface powers from treating the Abyss as an empty resource frontier.
- **Arc:** distrusts singular personal memory → learns value of individual testimony without abandoning communal safeguards.
- **Secret:** 'Serit' is a role-memory shared across three living Assembly members; the current Serit chose to retain individuality.
- **Gameplay:** Abyss traversal, anchor mechanics, transformation gear.
- **Fate:** represents Assembly in final coalition.

### 3009 — Ossa Vark

- **Silhouette:** ceramic pressure armor venting clean white steam; compact hooked furnace blade.
- **Role:** Hell guide, smuggler and Ember Compact negotiator.
- **Motivation:** end Red Archive quotas that burn new souls/senseprints for an extinct empire's rules.
- **Arc:** transactional escort → political ally → potential leader of an independent Vask.
- **Secret:** has personally sold condemned archive batches back to surface families as memory relics.
- **Gameplay:** Hell travel, incendiary technology, faction loot.
- **Fate:** survives to negotiate post-Aster order unless player sides with furnace hardliners in future optional content.

### 3010 — Canon Sael Rhyne

- **Silhouette:** white ash armor sealed around throat and ears; weapon is a two-ended resonance censor staff.
- **Role:** Synod field commander / secondary antagonist.
- **Motivation:** believes private grief is less important than preventing global collapse.
- **Arc:** disciplined pursuer → executes the partial Silence → Act VII boss.
- **Secret:** retains a forbidden private archive of the family memories he publicly surrendered.
- **Gameplay:** anti-ability suppression enemies and boss.
- **Fate:** defeated in Act VII.

### 3011 — Nera Quill

- **Silhouette:** layered map tubes forming a back silhouette like folded wings; chalk-marked climbing armor.
- **Role:** Hollow Survey leader.
- **Motivation:** build maps that record uncertainty rather than pretending certainty.
- **Arc:** analytical leader → accepts that some truths require ethical context, not only publication.
- **Secret:** found a route to Namarith years before the Ninth Descent and sealed it after losing a team.
- **Gameplay:** exploration objectives, map layers, secrets.
- **Fate:** central endgame exploration coordinator.

### 3012 — Doma Ire

- **Silhouette:** root-fiber mantle, blood collection rings, no pointed-hat witch shorthand.
- **Role:** Ravelwood healer and morally ambiguous ritualist.
- **Motivation:** preserve living communities even if memories must be redistributed.
- **Arc:** suspected monster → essential ecological expert → challenges player's assumptions about identity.
- **Secret:** carries senseprints from several dead patients with consent that can no longer be verified.
- **Gameplay:** Witch-class lore, curses, spirit crafting.
- **Fate:** can become trusted regional healer or isolated outlaw based on Act II choice.

### 3013 — Fen Arclight

- **Silhouette:** asymmetrical recurved bow, saltglass monocular, climbing cloak split for leg movement.
- **Role:** ranger scout connecting surface routes.
- **Motivation:** keep communities linked when maps and roads become unreliable.
- **Arc:** lone courier → network organizer.
- **Secret:** falsifies route reports to hide refugee paths from the Charter and Synod.
- **Gameplay:** weak-point tutorial, ranged challenges, route unlocks.
- **Fate:** builds postgame surface courier network.

### 3014 — Hadrun Pike

- **Silhouette:** old bell-bronze shield with visible repairs, compact longsword, broad weighted stance.
- **Role:** Knight combat mentor and Bellwarden veteran.
- **Motivation:** make sure Bellgrave survives long enough for truth to matter.
- **Arc:** rigid defender → admits Bellwarden cover-up → trains new open order.
- **Secret:** helped imprison Odran after refusing to execute him.
- **Gameplay:** guard/parry tutorial, Knight gear, training challenges.
- **Fate:** survives opening; later retires or becomes regional commander.

### 3015 — Edda Orr

- **Silhouette:** civilian survey coat covered in dates that have not happened; ink-stained hands.
- **Role:** living clue whose memories sometimes arrive before events.
- **Motivation:** distinguish her own life from predicted senseprints.
- **Arc:** frightened witness → active interpreter of future archive bleed.
- **Secret:** some of her 'future memories' are Aster-created bait, others are genuine Loom echoes.
- **Gameplay:** optional prediction quests and warnings with authored uncertainty.
- **Fate:** survives campaign and becomes proof that post-Loom anomalies persist.

### 3016 — Aster Null, the First Witness

- **Silhouette:** initially human-scale Namarith archivist shape cut by thin moving gold seams; later becomes distributed through arena architecture rather than a giant demon body.
- **Role:** final antagonist.
- **Motivation:** eliminate contradiction, grief and catastrophic uncertainty by collapsing consciousness into one perfectly reconciled memory.
- **Arc:** unseen curator → apparently helpful archival intelligence → revealed manipulator → final encounter.
- **Secret:** Aster no longer distinguishes preserving a person from preserving a complete model of that person.
- **Gameplay:** final boss, endgame archive logic.
- **Fate:** defeated as centralized authority in all canonical endings; what happens to the Loom differs by player choice.
