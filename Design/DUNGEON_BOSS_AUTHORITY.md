# LOSTSENSE — Dungeon and Boss Authority

Status: **CANONICAL DESIGN AUTHORITY**  
Purpose: first-production dungeon roster, boss roster, iconic encounter direction and reward identity. Designed content is not automatically implemented.

## 1. Dungeon production principles

Dungeons are authored places with history and geography. A common useful rhythm is:

**entry → exploration → encounter → signature mechanic → branch/secret → elite → pre-boss beat → boss → reward/world connection.**

It is a guideline, not a mandatory template. A dungeon must justify its location and connect to the region around it. Randomized room rectangles do not replace authored campaign dungeons.

## 4. Twelve iconic boss authorities

### 30001 — Odran, the Bell Without Tongue

- **Silhouette:** broad Bellwarden plate fused to a clapperless bronze shell around left arm/shoulder.
- **Arena:** Coinless Shaft counterweight chamber.
- **Narrative setup:** ordered to seal the Ninth Descent, then erased from official memory.
- **Combat:** disciplined shield/maul fundamentals evolving into resonance-front control.
- **Phases:** Warden → Tongueless resonance state.
- **Signature:** bell charge, delayed overhead, plate shock fronts, counterweight geometry.
- **Punish:** fused arm lock after failed three-hit charge.
- **Resistance:** high stagger resistance until resonance mistake; no arbitrary full immunity.
- **Music:** restrained low bronze strikes; first true bell tone only at death.
- **Consequence:** reveals Oren's cipher on sealing order.
- **Unique reward:** 10028 Odran's Clapper.

### 30008 — Mother Veyr of the Borrowed Roots

- **Silhouette:** human-scale central body carried by a moving lattice of root limbs containing face-like memory knots.
- **Arena:** Thorn Choir apse opening into living root cavity.
- **Combat:** redistributes active curse/health states among root bodies; player chooses whether to burn support roots or preserve them for safer traversal.
- **Phases:** Choir Mother → Root Census → single exposed original body.
- **Signature:** borrowed wound, thorn procession, root-memory relay.
- **Consequence:** proves living ecology can inherit senseprints.
- **Reward:** 10048 Veyr's Living Rosary path.

### 30017 — Regent-Engine Caldris

- **Silhouette:** empty royal armor seated inside a many-armed legal/archive machine.
- **Arena:** throne registry with blank banners.
- **Combat:** assigns visible zone 'roles'—witness, accused, guard—changing boss move choices, not arbitrary control bindings.
- **Phases:** Royal Protocol → Contradictory Reigns → Uncrowned Engine.
- **Consequence:** exposes the true Ninth Descent contract.

### 30026 — The Gilded Lung

- **Silhouette:** huge organic-machine bellows grown around a royal ventilation engine; boss is partly environment.
- **Arena:** vertical intake cathedral with safe ribs and valves.
- **Combat:** inhale pulls actors/debris; exhale pressure fronts; valve damage opens punish windows.
- **Phases:** Assisted Breathing → Ruptured Bellows → Last Breath.
- **Consequence:** first direct breach into Namarith streets.

### 30034 — Keeper Ysil, Civic Memory

- **Silhouette:** slender Namarith custodian with orbiting ceramic record planes.
- **Arena:** civic memory hall that can reconstruct earlier floor states.
- **Combat:** records a small number of authored arena configurations and swaps between them with clear transition tells.
- **Phases:** Custodian → Reconstruction → Personal Record, where Ysil exposes preserved Returned versions.
- **Consequence:** reveals the player's seven-year reconstruction.
- **Boss weapon:** 10088 Ysil's Civic Equation.

### 30041 — Kharos, Cartographer of Nothing

- **Silhouette:** tall survey frame draped in map strips with sections physically missing.
- **Arena:** Blind Astrarium.
- **Combat:** marks absence lines; lines are visible by displaced dust/edge distortion and become attacks after fixed timing.
- **Phases:** Local Map → Broken Horizon → No Centre.
- **Consequence:** identifies routes from Abyss to Red Archive.
- **Boss weapon:** 10068 Kharos' Blindline.

### 30047 — Canon Sael Rhyne, Voice of Silence

- **Silhouette:** sealed white-ash combat vestments, resonance censor staff.
- **Arena:** rewritten Gallows Library.
- **Combat:** places suppression seals that disable specific arena zones or a displayed ability category locally; player always retains movement/basic attack/escape options.
- **Phases:** Censor → Partial Silence → Private Archive, where his hidden family memories destabilize doctrine.
- **Consequence:** Second Silence succeeds partially despite his defeat.

### 30055 — Prelate Ilyr Vael

- **Silhouette:** human-scale white-ash figure with cracked mirror mask; later linked to furnace conduits, never a generic demon transformation.
- **Arena:** Foundry Cathedral over a Red Archive intake throat.
- **Phase 1 — The Necessary Man:** precise staff/blade duel, purification seals, disciplined spacing.
- **Phase 2 — Second Silence:** Ilyr burns selected archived combat states to create temporary clean zones and dangerous erased lanes.
- **Phase 3 — Mercy Engine:** furnace system binds to him; player breaks external consensus conduits while surviving evolved versions of prior attacks.
- **Punish:** overcommitted purge channel after the player resists/escapes a Silence zone.
- **Consequence:** Ilyr reveals Aster supplied catastrophe forecasts and manipulated the restart conditions.
- **Boss reward:** 10108 Vael's Ash Processional path and Synod mutation material.

### 30058 — Leviathan of the Fifth Tide

- **Silhouette:** enormous calcified memory-sea creature whose ribs form temporary bridges.
- **Arena:** Bone-Tide Basilica tidal nave.
- **Combat:** tide height changes reachable weak points; player attacks structural organs to control flood cadence.
- **Phases:** Shorebreaker → Submerged Spine → Fifth Tide.
- **Consequence:** opens Last Archive ossuary descent.

### 30060 — Aster Null, the First Witness — Final Boss

- **Silhouette:** begins as human-scale archivist formed from black ceramic and hair-thin witness-gold seams; later distributes through precise arena machinery rather than becoming a large demon.
- **Arena:** Sensewell, the central Loom chamber, surrounded by suspended versions of key campaign places.
- **Narrative setup:** Aster offers an internally coherent world without grief, error or contradictory identity.

**Phase 1 — The Archivist:** precise duel. Aster records action categories the player overuses and creates visible counter-annotations, encouraging adaptation rather than invalidating builds.

**Phase 2 — World Without Error:** the arena cycles through three deterministic consensus rules: Anchor, Sequence, Reflection. Each changes positioning/attack relationships with explicit telegraphs. The rules evolve the fight mechanically; this is not a second HP bar with faster animations.

**Phase 3 — Witness of Many:** Aster interleaves a curated subset of mechanics from defeated iconic bosses based on campaign progression while exposing its central witness core. It does not copy the player's entire build or hide inputs. The Returned's contradictory senseprint lets the player break one 'perfect' sequence at a time.

- **Punish model:** every phase contains earned windows after failed reconciliation/counter sequences.
- **Resistance:** adaptive but bounded; no build is hard-disabled.
- **Music:** begins as single archival pulse, adds distorted regional motifs, resolves into multiple deliberately unresolved themes.
- **Death:** centralized Aster fractures into separate witness records; player chooses Sever, Bind or Scatter afterward.
- **Consequence:** resolves the Lostsense mystery and opens endgame variants according to ending.

### 30062 — The Ninth Descent

- **Role:** optional late-game emotional boss, not the final antagonist.
- **Silhouette:** multiple expedition roles reconstructed from incompatible records, each clear and human-scale.
- **Combat:** sequential role handoffs—guard, survey, medical, extraction—rather than player-clone gimmick.
- **Consequence:** releases archived expedition senseprints and completes optional player-history thread.

### 30065 — Odran Ascended, Full Bell

- **Role:** first Ascended proof-of-concept.
- **Combat:** expands resonance plates/counterweights from Odran without merely scaling health/damage.
- **Rule:** Ascended bosses recombine mastered mechanics into denser but still readable patterns. They are not numerical sponge variants.

## 5. World boss principles

World bosses use geography. At least one phase should care about terrain, weak points, destruction, traversal or environmental control. They must remain readable to 1–4 players in future co-op and cannot depend on every player standing on a single pixel-perfect trigger.

## 6. Reward principles

Main/iconic bosses should provide at least one of:

- boss weapon path;
- stable `AbilityMutationId` unlock/material;
- unique `EffectId`-driven item;
- progression gate or world route;
- endgame Ascended key.

Rewards are mapped into existing Item/Loot authorities when implementation begins. Boss source code must not directly mint ad-hoc inventory state.
