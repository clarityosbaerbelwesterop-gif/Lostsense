# LOSTSENSE — World Authority

Status: **CANONICAL DESIGN AUTHORITY**  
World: **Avarra**

## 1. World topology

Avarra is geographically continuous. Major region changes correspond to real surface distance, depth, geology and old infrastructure. Fast travel may exist later, but the canonical spatial relationships remain physical.

Core descent:

**Surface settlements / forests / ruins → caverns → Vaur gold network → royal deep mines → Namarith buried civilization → Abyss → Red Archive/Hell → Last Archive.**

Players should repeatedly see evidence of places above and below: roots breaking through mine roofs, old city foundations visible in caverns, royal boreholes cutting through Namarith streets, Hell exhaust stacks venting into Abyss rock and stabilization bells mounted over shafts whose chains disappear kilometres downward.

## 3. Signature Gold Mine network

The Vaur network is a world beneath the world, not one dungeon.

**Upper Vaur Goldworks → Abandoned Industrial Shafts → Royal Excavation → Forbidden Deep Mine → Namarith Pre-Human Excavations → Abyss Breach.**

Depth changes must be readable through production language:

- Upper layers: timber, iron rails, practical lamps, recent labor camps.
- Royal layers: stone drilling halls, heraldry, huge pumps, failed gilded machinery.
- Forbidden layers: collapsed geometry, miners' notes contradicting floor plans, witness-gold blooms.
- Namarith layers: human-era tunnels literally cut through older streets and civic machines.
- Breach layer: architecture stops assuming gravity and distance behave consistently.

The mine network connects Bellgrave, Ravelwood, Giltfen and Crownless Reach underground so later shortcuts make early geography feel newly understood.

## 4. The Abyss

The Abyss is the physical deficit created by the Loom's attempt to reconcile impossible versions of reality. It has pressure, ecology, trade and inhabitants.

### Physical rules

- Stable matter requires an **anchor relationship**: position relative to a trusted beacon, structure or living group.
- Long-unobserved pathways can shift between visits, but authored critical routes remain understandable.
- Sound transmission is unreliable in the Choirless Expanse, creating a cultural emphasis on light, vibration and touch rather than an accessibility-hostile audio gimmick.

### Quiet Assembly

The **Quiet Assembly** is not a monster faction. Its people have survived for generations by distributing memory across communal witness objects so no single person carries all identity. They view surface individuality as beautiful but dangerously fragile.

### Resources

- **Nullstone:** resists senseprint retention.
- **Pale filament:** flexible mineral-organic threads used in high-tier defensive equipment.
- **Unvoiced gold:** witness gold deliberately stripped of stored experience, prized for precise magical work.

## 5. Hell / the Red Archive

Hell is a gigantic late-game territory created from Namarith's discard layer. It burns corrupted and rejected senseprints but has evolved beyond that function.

It is politically divided. The **Ember Compact** wants independence from the ancient Loom and surface religions. Old furnace intelligences want to resume their assigned destruction quotas. The Candescent Synod treats Hell as proof that unwanted memory can be purged.

Hell is not uniformly molten. Its major expressions are ash deserts, industrial cities, pressure tunnels, slag deltas, ceramic cathedrals and black archive seas.

## 6. Settlements and social geography

Primary safe/social anchors for initial production:

- **Bellgrave:** opening settlement and vertical-slice hub.
- **Morrowstep:** Ravelwood timber village built on root stilts.
- **Hush Quay:** Giltfen pump-town and alchemy market.
- **Crownmarket:** survivor quarter inside Crownless Reach.
- **Breakwater Nine:** Saltglass coastal refuge.
- **Chainwatch:** Stormspine fortress settlement.
- **Quiet House of Bearings:** first Abyss social anchor.
- **Ember Court:** Vask neutral trade enclave.

Settlements should change visibly after major acts instead of functioning as timeless menu spaces.

## 7. Vertical route rules

Every major region must expose at least one believable downward route and one late shortcut upward where geography supports it. Vertical transitions are authored travel spaces when practical: mine cages, root shafts, drains, civic lifts, boreholes, fault shelves and furnace elevators.

Portals are reserved for specific Lostsense/Namarith phenomena, not used as the default explanation for level changes.
