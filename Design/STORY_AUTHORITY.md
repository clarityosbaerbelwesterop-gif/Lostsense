# LOSTSENSE — Story Authority

Status: **CANONICAL DESIGN AUTHORITY**  
Campaign structure: nine acts, start-to-finish, with postgame opening.

## 1. Narrative premise

Seven years after the **Ninth Descent** disappeared below Bellgrave, one expedition member climbs from a shaft that has been sealed since the disaster: the player, now called **the Returned Surveyor**. Their memories stop at the expedition's third day. Their body carries no obvious seven-year aging, yet Namarith machines buried far below already contain their senseprint.

At the same time, Lostsense events accelerate across Avarra. Roads remember erased destinations, the dead repeat unfamiliar lives and deep witness-gold veins begin transmitting events that have not happened yet.

The player descends first to discover what happened to the Ninth Descent, then to prevent the world from being forcibly rewritten.

## 2. Campaign truths

These reveals are fixed canon:

1. Witness gold stores senseprints; it does not merely amplify magic.
2. The Ninth Descent was secretly directed toward a Namarith interface, not an ordinary royal ore survey.
3. Archivist Oren Sile knew this and selected the Returned because their senseprint showed unusual stability.
4. The ancient Namarith built the Loom Below to preserve shared truth and eventually attempted to eliminate contradiction.
5. The Abyss is the deficit left by that failed reconciliation.
6. Hell / the Red Archive is an engineered discard layer that became a world.
7. Ilyr Vael's Second Silence can reduce Lostsense, but only by erasing enormous amounts of living identity.
8. Aster Null encouraged both the Ninth Descent and the Second Silence to create enough synchronized senseprints to restart the Loom.
9. The Returned survived seven years because a broken Loom process repeatedly reconstructed them from competing archived versions.
10. The Returned is not a chosen god or hidden monarch. Their importance comes from being a living unresolved contradiction the Loom cannot safely normalize.

## 3. Act I — The Bell That Forgot

**Regions:** Bellgrave March, Ravelwood edge, Upper Vaur Goldworks.  
**Primary dungeon:** Coinless Shaft (20,003).  
**Main boss:** Odran, the Bell Without Tongue (30,001).

### Opening state

Bellgrave's largest stabilization bell rings at dawn, but no one except Mara Venn remembers hearing it. Minutes later the Returned climbs from a sealed mine cage beneath the belfry.

### Main quest chain

| Quest ID | Quest | Production objective |
|---:|---|---|
| 80001 | The Returned | Character creation/class memory; reach Bellgrave alive; meet Mara Venn. |
| 80002 | A Bell Without Sound | Investigate the belfry and prove the town has lost a shared event. |
| 80003 | Marks on the Ninth Cage | Recover the expedition plate from Weeping Cut and establish the first combat/traversal route. |
| 80004 | Debt of the Foreman | Find Ivo Salk; learn the Ninth Descent was rerouted after departure. |
| 80005 | The Coinless Shaft | Enter the sealed mine, restore a cage brake, defeat its elite warden and descend. |
| 80006 | Tongueless | Defeat Odran, recover his bell core and witness the first coherent senseprint playback. |

### Reveal

Odran was a Bellwarden captain ordered to seal the Ninth Descent below and kill anyone who returned. His memory shows the command came with Oren Sile's archive cipher.

### World change / unlock

Bellgrave's central bell becomes a stable hub anchor. The player gains full skill allocation and equipment crafting access. Deeper mine gates open.

## 12. Ending authority

The ending is a player choice earned after Aster's defeat, not a dialogue shortcut before the fight.

### Sever

Destroy the Loom's ability to impose consensus. Lostsense becomes locally unpredictable but no longer centrally weaponizable. The world keeps conflicting memory and individual identity.

### Bind

The Returned becomes the Loom's finite living mediator. Major Lostsense events reduce, but the protagonist permanently bears the burden of reconciling disputes. Endgame explores threats attempting to capture this authority.

### Scatter

Break the Loom into many bounded regional anchors controlled by living communities, Quiet Assembly protocols and Ember Compact safeguards. Stability becomes political and imperfect rather than centralized.

All endings support postgame through surviving deep anomalies, Ascended encounters, faction consequences and unreconciled archive zones.
